Vision

I build at the intersection of advocacy and entrepreneurship — translating systems, languages, and ideas so immigrant communities and young people can access care, culture, and opportunity without barriers.

As founder of Fluent in Minds Society and a partner across mental health and civic organizations, I treat language access as infrastructure: measurable, scalable, and designed with the people it serves.
